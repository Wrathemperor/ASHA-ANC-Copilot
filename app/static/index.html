Antenatal Clinical Guidelines & Referral Triggers

HYPERTENSION & PRE-ECLAMPSIA

Blood Pressure Stratification

  • Normal: < 140/90 mmHg (Routine ANC follow-up)
  • Stage 1 (Gestational HTN): 140–159 / 90–109 mmHg → Clinician review within 48h
  • Severe Preeclampsia / Crisis: ≥ 160/110 mmHg OR any HTN with headache, vision blur, or epigastric pain → Urgent 108 Hospital Transfer
ANEMIA & NUTRITION

Hemoglobin (Hb) Cutoffs

  • Normal: ≥ 11.0 g/dL (Daily IFA + Calcium tablets)
  • Mild Anemia: 10.0 – 10.9 g/dL (Double IFA therapeutic dose)
  • Moderate Anemia: 7.0 – 9.9 g/dL → ANM/MO investigation
  • Severe Anemia: < 7.0 g/dL → Urgent FRU / District Hospital referral for IV iron/blood transfusion
EMERGENCY BYPASS

12 PMSMA Red-Flag Danger Signs

  • Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy (Rule BLEED-01)
  • Convulsions / fits / loss of consciousness (Rule CONV-01)
  • Severe persistent headache + blurred vision (Rule HTN-03)
  • Reduced or absent fetal movements in 3rd trimester (Rule FETAL-01)
  • Sudden fluid leakage / ruptured membranes (Rule MEMBRANE-01)
  • High fever with chills or foul vaginal discharge (Rule SEPSIS-01)
SAFETY PRINCIPLE

Tri-State Clinical Safety Doctrine

Silence ≠ Denial: A symptom is marked Present only when explicitly stated, and Denied only when explicitly refuted. All unmentioned symptoms remain strictly Unknown until directly investigated.

Zero Hallucination: System generates administrative referral slips and guideline citations only; clinician retains all diagnostic authority.
